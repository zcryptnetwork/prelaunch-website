More

More you can do.

Any token. Any chain.ZCrypt is built to carry any token from any chain. Bitcoin is live. Ethereum, USDT, DAI, Solana and more are on the roadmap, and each becomes a private asset once it is here. No exchange needed.Swap one coin for another directly with someone. Nobody holds your money in the middle. A swap's amounts, coin types and one-time keys are public for now. Built to resist quantum computers.STARK proofs, hash-based ownership, ML-KEM encryption, ML-DSA signatures. No elliptic curves in a payment, from the first block. Make your own coin.No Solidity, no smart contract. Pick a ticker and a supply, click Mint, and the coin is yours. Bitcoin, in and out.Lock Bitcoin and get zBTC, one for one and private. The network checks every deposit on Bitcoin itself. Redeem it for Bitcoin whenever you like.
