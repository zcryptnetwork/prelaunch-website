01 — 06 Why this is different

01

Nobody can see
your money.

On most blockchains every balance and every payment is public forever. On ZCrypt nothing is. Not as an option you switch on. Always. Your coins sit in one giant crowd with everyone else's, and a payment cannot be picked out of that crowd.

Who paid, who got paid, how much, which coin: none of it is on the chain.

02

Hide in the whole crowd,
not a small one.

Other privacy coins hide you among a handful of decoys, and over time patterns leak through. On ZCrypt every payment hides among every coin that has ever existed. The crowd only gets bigger, and it never thins out.

Even the number of coins you spent is hidden.

03

Cash is
a file.

A payment is a tiny text file. Send it on WhatsApp, email, AirDrop, or print it out. The first person to open it in their wallet gets the money. No address. No account. They don't even need to own any coins yet.

zcrypt-bearer.txt
zcrypt-bearer/1
coin zcb1q8f3l…m7d2kx0v
gas  zcb1zr9ap…4hqn6te8

It works like cash. First one to the bank cashes it. Delete your copy after you send it.

04

Trade without
an exchange.

No company has to list your coin, and no company can freeze it or pull the plug. Two people agree on a trade, both lock in what they're offering, and it settles for both at the same moment or not at all. Swap ZCRYPT for any coin on the chain. Bring Bitcoin in, too.

Find your counterparty anywhere. The trade itself never leaves ZCrypt.

05

Still private when
quantum computers arrive.

Most blockchains rest on math that quantum computers are expected to break. ZCrypt's money doesn't. It is built on the new generation of cryptography that governments are switching to right now. A payment you make today stays secret decades from now.

No signatures anywhere near your coins. Nothing for a quantum computer to crack.

06

Don't trust us.
Check it.

Private money needs public rules. Every payment carries proof that it passed seven simple checks, and you can watch those checks on any transaction in the explorer. The rule that says nobody can print money out of thin air isn't a promise from a team. A computer has proven it.

  1. Amounts are real
  2. One coin type per payment
  3. Nothing created, nothing lost
  4. Spent coins are spent
  5. New coins match
  6. Every coin came from the pool
  7. Done

Open any transaction in the explorer and watch the checks run.

Make your own coin

Pick a name, a supply, and go. Your coin gets the same privacy as ZCRYPT itself: send it, trade it, or hand it to someone as a file. On the chain it looks exactly like every other payment. Nobody can tell what moved.

Show your accountant. Only your accountant.

You can give someone a view-only key that reveals what came into one of your addresses, and nothing more. They can't spend. They can't see what you did next. You can revoke it any time by moving on.

Proven, not promised

Hidden money is only as good as its rules. The rule that nothing can be created out of thin air has been checked line by line by a proof assistant. Where the proofs stop is written down too, in plain language.

Download Walk me through it

Checking the latest release.

    1. 1
      Install it. Mac: open the disk image. Windows: run the setup. Linux: use the AppImage or the deb.
    2. 2
      Connect to the network. Settings → Consensus Node → https://rpc.zcrypt.network.
    3. 3
      Tor is on by default. If you don't run Tor, switch it off in Settings for your first session.
    4. 4
      Get paid with nothing in your wallet. Someone sends you coins or a bearer file, you hit Refresh, and it's yours. Each payment you send costs 0.01 ZCRYPT.

    Mac builds are signed and notarized by Apple. The Windows installer is not yet signed, so SmartScreen may warn you. Want to build it yourself? The developer docs show how.
