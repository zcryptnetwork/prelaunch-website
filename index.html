What is ZCrypt

Cash, but for the internet.

Today's digital payments come with hidden costs: surveillance, censorship, and fragile security built on cryptography that quantum computers will eventually break. ZCrypt is designed from the ground up to be different.

Private by default

Amounts, senders, and recipients are hidden using Poseidon commitments. Privacy isn't an option you have to remember to enable — it's how the protocol works.

Instant verification

Each coin carries a recursive zero-knowledge proof that validates its entire history. Your wallet verifies it locally in under two milliseconds — no blockchain sync required.

Quantum-resistant

Built with WOTS+ hash-based signatures that quantum computers can't break. Every coin is protected today against the computers of tomorrow.

Works like cash

Send a coin file as easily as sending a photo. The recipient verifies it instantly, offline if needed. No accounts, no intermediaries, no approval required.
