Trading

No exchange holds your money.

An exchange does two jobs: it matches buyers with sellers, and it holds everyone's money while they trade. ZCrypt does the second job itself. A trade settles on the chain, directly between the two people, in a few seconds.

01

The chain settles the trade.

On an ordinary exchange you deposit first. From then on the exchange holds your coins, and a trade is a change in its private records. If it is hacked, frozen or goes bankrupt, your coins go with it.

On ZCrypt nobody takes a deposit. When two orders match, each person locks what they are selling on the chain. The locks are tied together: collecting one side releases the other. Either both people are paid or both get their coins back.

The trading engine only introduces buyer and seller. It never touches the money.

Under the hood
  • Each side opens a hash-locked escrow under the same SHA-256 hash. Only one side knows the secret behind it.
  • Each escrow names the other one exactly, with its coin, amount and keys, so a lock cannot be paired with anything else.
  • Claiming one escrow publishes the secret, which is what lets the other side claim theirs.
  • If the secret is never revealed, both escrows expire and each side is refunded.
  • A block takes one second and is final, so a trade is two locks and a claim: about two to three seconds.

02

What that makes possible.

An engine with no vault

Anyone can run an order book like the one above. It needs no custody, no hot wallet and no withdrawal queue, because it never holds a coin.

Any coin, one rule

ZCRYPT, zBTC and every coin issued on ZCrypt trade the same way. A new coin can be traded the moment it exists.

Bitcoin too

The same lock works across to Bitcoin, which is how Bitcoin comes in and out as zBTC.

In the wallet today

The wallet can already swap one coin for another with someone directly. You agree a price, and the wallet does the locking and claiming.

03

What is true today.

  • There is no order book on the chain. Prices are agreed off the chain, between two people or by an engine someone runs. ZCrypt does the settlement.
  • The chain is already launched, with swaps enabled. The trading platform itself arrives in Q2 2027. The screen above is a sample of it.
  • A trade is less private than a payment. The two locks are public: the amounts, the coin types and a one-time key for each side. The wallet uses a fresh key for every trade, so the locks do not point at your address. Hiding the amounts is planned.
  • A trade costs 0.01 or 0.02 ZCRYPT in network fees, destroyed like every other fee. Nobody takes a cut.
