The mark, the colour,
the type. Use them as they are.

ZCrypt is drawn once and used everywhere. The source files below are the same ones the wallets ship with. Take what you need for an article, an app listing or an integration; don't redraw, recolour or add effects.

The mark

A Z in one stroke with round ends. On the signal gradient it is the tile: app icon and avatar. Alone it takes the text colour, or cyan on ink. Clear space is one bar-height on every side; the minimum size is 16 px.

The wordmark

ZCrypt
  • ZCrypt is the project and the wallet. Capital Z, capital C.
  • ZCRYPT in capitals is the coin. 1,000 ZCRYPT.
  • zcrypt-1 in mono is the network.
  • Set in Inter Tight 600 at −0.02em tracking, never stretched or outlined.

Shape and motion

  • 18px radius on cards, sheets and buttons. 12px on inputs and chips. Pills are fully round.
  • The tile's corner radius is 27.5% of its side.
  • One easing, cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1), and nothing moves for people who turned motion off.
  • The cyan glow is for the tile and the balance card. Not for text, not for borders.
  • Buttons say what happens: "Send", then "Sent". No shouting capitals.
