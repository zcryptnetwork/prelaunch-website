ZCrypt Wallet Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Echelon Labs LLC

ZCrypt Wallet is a self-custodial wallet for the ZCrypt network. It is built so that we learn nothing about you. This page explains what the app stores, what it sends, and to whom.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and no tracking of any kind. We never receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, device identifiers, or wallet contents.

What stays on your device

  • Your 24-word recovery phrase and spending keys, sealed with Argon2id and ChaCha20-Poly1305 under your PIN. When Face ID or Touch ID is enabled, the device secret is kept in the iOS Keychain. It never leaves the device.
  • Your coins, balance and payment history, stored in the app's private sandbox.
  • Your settings, including the node address you choose to connect to.

Files you export (bearer coins, stealth notes, receipts) are written to the app's Documents folder and shared only when you choose to share them.

What the app sends over the network

To work, a wallet has to talk to the blockchain. ZCrypt Wallet connects to:

  • A ZCrypt node, by default rpc.zcrypt.network operated by Echelon Labs LLC. You can point the app at any node, including your own or one reached over Tor. The node receives the shielded transactions you submit and answers queries for new blocks. A ZCrypt transaction reveals no sender, recipient, amount or asset. Like any internet server, the node can see the IP address of the connection; we do not log or retain IP addresses for wallet connections.
  • For the optional Bitcoin bridge, a public Bitcoin block explorer API (by default blockstream.info, operated by Blockstream) to read Bitcoin transactions. That service's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

No other network requests are made. The app does not contact Echelon Labs LLC for any purpose other than relaying to the node above.

Face ID and Touch ID

Biometric checks are performed entirely by iOS. The app only receives a yes or no answer and never sees biometric data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. Keep your recovery phrase safe: it is the only way to restore your wallet.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
