Any token, any chain

Any token. Any chain.

ZCrypt is built to work with any token from any chain. Bitcoin is live today. Ethereum, USDT, DAI, Solana and others are on the roadmap. Once an asset is on ZCrypt, it is private.

01

Bring it in. It becomes private.

Most chains are public. Every balance and every transfer of ETH, USDT or SOL can be read by anyone, forever. ZCrypt does not ask you to give those assets up. It lets you hold and send them in private.

An asset that comes in from its home chain gets a counterpart on ZCrypt with a z in front: Bitcoin becomes zBTC, Ether becomes zETH, USDT becomes zUSDT. Each one is backed one for one by the original, locked on its own chain, and can be turned back whenever you like.

The same asset, the same value, held in private.

03

How a chain is onboarded.

Bitcoin shows the pattern. ZCrypt does not trust a committee to say that a deposit happened. It checks the other chain itself.

  1. Lock. You lock the asset on its home chain.
  2. Prove. ZCrypt's validators verify that deposit against the home chain's own blocks, inside consensus.
  3. Mint. The same amount is created on ZCrypt, one for one, straight into the private pool.
  4. Redeem. To leave, you lock the z-asset, receive the original on its home chain, and the z-asset is destroyed.

For Bitcoin this is running now: a Bitcoin light client inside ZCrypt's consensus checks every deposit. Each new chain gets the same treatment, with a verifier for that chain's own rules. That work is what the roadmap is.
