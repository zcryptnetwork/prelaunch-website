Architecture

Two processes per node: a Rust ABCI state machine (impl/abci-node) and CometBFT 0.38 for BFT consensus and networking. Blocks target 1 second and are final on commit. The full consensus state is persisted as an atomic snapshot at every commit and resumes exactly (same height, same app hash) on restart.

The app hash commits to every ledger: the note-commitment tree root, the nullifier sparse-Merkle root, the staking ledger (validators, delegations, rewards, foundation vesting progress, unbonding queue, tombstones), the asset registry, the HTLC escrow book, and the validator set. Two nodes that disagree about anything disagree about the app hash and cannot both finalize.

The 0x55 bundle

tag ‖ gas_blind(64) ‖ gas_len(4) ‖ gas_tx ‖ main_tx. The gas note is a small ZCRYPT shield tx whose output[0] is a burn of exactly 0.01 ZCRYPT to an unspendable key; publishing gas_blind makes that single output publicly checkable (every node recomputes the commitment) while everything else stays hidden. This is how a transfer of a hidden non-ZCRYPT asset still provably pays ZCRYPT gas. Both halves verify and apply atomically.

Native ZCRYPT gas: 0x6A and 0x6B

A ZCRYPT transfer does not need the bundle. 0x6A ‖ burn_value(8) ‖ burn_blind(64) ‖ shield_tx is a single shield tx whose output[0] is a revealed burn of burn_value ≥ 0.01 ZCRYPT to the unspendable key. A spend is single-asset, so the revealed ZCRYPT burn pins every hidden input and output to ZCRYPT, and the burn is the gas. The circuit has three outputs (burn, payment, change), so a wallet whose entire balance is one coin can pay the fee, pay the recipient and keep its change in one proof. Dust below the fee rides along with a later coin (the burn may exceed 0.01 so no dust change coin has to be kept).

A swap leg can also skip the gas note. 0x6B is the 0x62 header followed by a 32-byte sponsor_ref, the two settlement openings and the asset spend. A ZCRYPT leg burns its gas as the spend's in-tx fee: 0.01, or 0.02 when it sponsors the other leg, in which case sponsor_ref names the counter-lock (its escrow id is a pure function of the terms, so it can be named before it exists) and the escrow records sponsor_for. A leg of any other asset pays nothing: it is admitted only when sponsor_ref names a ZCRYPT swap lock that is open, is the other leg of this swap (same hashlock, keys crossed), named exactly this lock, and has not sponsored before; admission marks that sponsorship used. Both facts are part of the escrow book and therefore of the state root. The wallet runs every swap that touches ZCRYPT ZCRYPT-first, so a wallet holding no ZCRYPT at all can swap into it: the ZCRYPT giver locks first and pays for both legs, the newcomer locks second with no gas, claims (claims are gas-free), and then owns a ZCRYPT coin that spends on its own via 0x6A.

Economics

  • Supply: 21,000,000 ZCRYPT, 6 decimals, at a 1-second target block time.
  • Mining (80% = 16.8M): block reward 0.14 ZCRYPT, halving every 60,000,000 blocks (~1.9 years), 63 halvings, integer dust never minted. Rewards pay out every 100-block epoch, weighted by (stake + delegated) x blocks signed.
  • Foundation (20% = 4.2M): vests by consensus rule as a pure function of height: 50,000 liquid at genesis, 25% cumulative at the month-12 cliff, then 87,500 per 30-day month through month 48. Claims beyond the vested amount are rejected in the mempool and in the block.
  • Fees: minimum 0.01 ZCRYPT on every value-moving action, always burned. Administrative releases (claim, unbond, undelegate) debit it from the value they release. Net supply therefore decreases with usage.
  • Bounds: max value per coin is 2^52 atomic units; larger claims split across coins automatically.

Staking

Bonding requires a minimum 10,000 ZCRYPT stake pledged by actually burning shielded coins (the bond carries a shield tx whose burn output consensus verifies against the declared stake). The validator set updates dynamically: a bond makes you a block producer, an unbond removes you, live. Delegators pledge the same way, earn pro-rata after the operator's commission, and share slashing risk. Unbonding and undelegating lock stake for 10 days, during which it remains slashable. Equivocation burns 5% of the offender's stake, and of the stake delegated to it, and tombstones the identity permanently. Accrued rewards convert to spendable shielded coins via 0x58 claims; the coins are reconstructible from the owner's seed alone.

Assets and issuance

Registration is first-mint-wins on the canonical ticker, with decimals and a hard supply cap fixed forever at registration. The mint authority is the commitment to an M-of-N ML-DSA key set: minting (via HTLC escrow) requires M valid signatures over the exact escrow parameters, and the on-chain supply counter enforces the cap. Authority rotation is itself threshold-authorized by the current set and epoch-bound against replay. Inside the pool, the asset id is hidden in each commitment; the circuit's single-asset and conservation constraints make cross-asset forgery impossible.

The Bitcoin bridge

Both chains can verify the same SHA-256 hashlock, so BTC and zBTC move by atomic swap. Minting zBTC needs no signature from anyone: consensus runs a Bitcoin light client, and a mint escrow opens only against an inclusion proof (SPV) of a Bitcoin deposit to the reserve. The threshold signature in the catalog above applies to other issued assets, not to zBTC.

  • BTC to zBTC: fund a P2WSH HTLC on Bitcoin; once validators have checked the deposit proof, a mint escrow opens on ZCrypt under the same hashlock; claiming either side reveals the preimage that settles the other. The zBTC mints into a commitment in the shielded pool. The owner key, value and asset are public at mint time. The beneficiary chooses the blind; an opening proof shows the commitment opens to that public value, asset, and their spend key, and the blind travels only in the encrypted note. While the escrow is retained the record names the settlement commitment, and the blind is never public. After prune a tree-only observer cannot recompute the leaf from the escrow id, but the open transaction still contains the commitment, so block history links the open to the leaf. The coin's later spend is unlinkable like any other coin.
  • zBTC to BTC: lock zBTC into a burn escrow; the bridge operator pays out on the BTC side from the reserve against the same preimage, and the lock refunds after its timeout if no payment comes; the claim records the burn, conserving supply 1:1.

The in-pool swap (0x62) reuses this machinery with two ZCrypt legs: two parties lock two different assets under one hashlock and one preimage settles both, atomically, with no on-chain order book. Pricing happens off-chain (RFQ). The chain sees two escrows and a reveal, and today those escrows are public: asset ids, amounts, hashlock, timeouts and both parties' keys are on chain, so the swap is atomic but not yet private (committed-value swaps are the planned fix; the wallet already uses fresh stealth keys per swap so the parties are not their payment addresses).

The bridge runs against Bitcoin mainnet. The Bitcoin reserve key is being placed under MPC, created in a key ceremony.

Chain identity and genesis

The chain id is compiled into the node, the transaction builders, and every proof transcript (cross-chain replay is impossible). At genesis the node registers the foundation's reward address: production builds require it via ZCRYPT_FOUNDATION_ADDR and refuse to start otherwise.
