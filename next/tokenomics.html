Tokenomics

Where every ZCRYPT comes from, who receives it, and what removes it. Each number on this page is a consensus rule that every validator enforces.

02

Block rewards.

16,800,000 ZCRYPT are paid out over time to the people who secure the network. Each block creates 0.14 ZCRYPT. The reward halves every 60,000,000 blocks, which is about every 1.9 years.

BlocksReward per blockPaid out in the period

Rewards are paid every 100 blocks to the validators that took part, and shared with anyone who stakes with them. A validator needs a stake of 10,000 ZCRYPT. Unstaking takes 10 days.

Half of all block rewards are paid in the first 1.9 years.

03

The foundation.

4,200,000 ZCRYPT are allocated to the foundation, Echelon Labs LLC, which builds ZCrypt. They are released on a schedule that validators enforce. A claim above what has vested is rejected by the network.

50,000 ZCRYPT have been claimed by the foundation so far. The explorer shows what has vested and what has been claimed.

04

Fees and yield.

Fees are destroyed

A payment to an address costs 0.01 ZCRYPT and a payment file 0.03. A swap costs 0.01 or 0.02. Claiming rewards and unstaking are free. Nobody receives a fee: it is removed from the supply for good, so use of the network makes ZCRYPT scarcer.

One fee coin

Payments in any coin on ZCrypt pay their fee in ZCRYPT.

Bitcoin yield

0.1% of every Bitcoin deposit is paid to stakers in zBTC, on top of block rewards.

Penalties

A validator that signs two conflicting blocks loses 5% of its stake, which is destroyed, and is removed for good.

The network launched on 28 October 2026. The same rules, as a reference table, are in the developer docs.
