    First steps

    Your first payment.

    1. Install it

      iPhone: get ZCrypt Wallet from the App Store. Mac: open the disk image. Windows: run the installer. Linux: use the AppImage or the deb.

    2. Write down your 24 words

      The wallet shows you a recovery phrase once. Write it on paper. It is the only way to get your money back if you lose the device, and anyone who has it can take your money.

    3. Get paid

      Someone sends you a payment file. Open it in the wallet and it is yours one block later. You do not need any coins first: the file pays its own fee.

    4. Get ZCRYPT

      Buy it with Bitcoin on the Buy tab of the desktop wallet, or have someone pay you. How to get ZCRYPT

    5. Pay someone

      Choose an amount, make a payment file, and send it in any chat. Or pay to an address: paste it, or scan its QR code. A payment to an address costs 0.01 ZCRYPT. A payment file costs 0.03, which covers the fee for the person who opens it.

    Good to know

    Before you start.

    • This is early software on a young network. Start with small amounts.
    • Tor is optional and off by default. Turn it on in Settings and the node no longer sees your IP address. It needs Tor running on your computer.
    • The desktop wallet connects to https://rpc.zcrypt.network. You can point it at your own node in Settings, under Consensus Node.
    • A payment file is cash. Until it is claimed, any copy of it can claim the money. Delete yours once you have sent it.
    • Check your download. Each release lists SHA256SUMS, and every installer can be verified as built from the public source: gh attestation verify FILE --repo echelonlabsresearch/ZCrypt

    Mac builds are signed and notarized by Apple. The Windows installer may show a SmartScreen warning. To build the wallet yourself, see the developer docs.
